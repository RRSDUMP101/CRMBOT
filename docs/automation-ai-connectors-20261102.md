# Automation, AI, reports, widget and external connectors — 2 November 2026

This document describes the implementation and its boundaries. It is not evidence of provider authorization, production flag activation, successful customer delivery, or completion of every Zaapi audit requirement. The deployment record owns actual version IDs and live checks. The earlier 1 November release document remains a historical record; its best-effort widget trigger limitation is superseded by migration020 and the durable ingress described here.

## Durable flows and scheduled broadcast

With `FLOWS_ENABLED=true`, website/widget, social and Sale-context ingress captures the event and matching published Flow versions in the same database commit as the canonical input. The bounded runner drains pending receipts, and execution keys deduplicate retries. A failed receipt uses backoff and becomes reviewable after ten attempts; an Admin can retry it from Flows. Turning the flag off does not backfill events received while disabled. Published-version capture preserves the version that matched the original event; stopping/unpublishing still fences execution.

Reply cancellation compares the committed customer message sequence against the sequence captured by a wait. An older delayed event cannot cancel a wait created for a later reply. Handoff and close clear the AI generation lease; required conversation fields are validated before closing. Waiting chats route through the operator routing hook after Flow assignment has had its opportunity to run. The editor includes Sale-context triggers, Instagram conditions, version comparisons, and ingress status/retry controls.

Broadcast needs both `BROADCAST_ENABLED=true` and `BROADCAST_RUNNER_ENABLED=true`. Each scheduled wake has a bounded campaign budget and retains the existing audience snapshot, active approver, consent recheck, pause/cancel and unknown-delivery safeguards. Backoff prevents a failing campaign from monopolizing the runner. Unknown provider submission is never automatically resent. WhatsApp templates are documented separately by their lifecycle module.

## AI knowledge and account activation

The AI workspace supports text, CSV, PDF text layers, DOCX and XLSX. Files are limited to 5 MB and extracted text to 100,000 characters. PDF extraction runs locally in the browser using the pinned, self-hosted PDF.js worker, with a 100-page cap. Image-only PDFs require OCR outside this implementation. Office parsing rejects entities, encrypted/unsupported archives, oversized expanded data, excessive XML depth and excessive archive entries; it extracts text, not document layout, formulas or images.

Website import accepts one HTTPS page on an exact operator-configured `AI_KNOWLEDGE_ALLOWED_HOSTS` hostname. Redirects, unlisted hosts, oversized responses and timeouts fail. It is not a recursive crawler. Configure only public hosts controlled or trusted by the organization.

Imported or edited content starts as a draft with immutable version history. An Admin reviews the exact version, expiry and allowed scopes before publication. Scope is the website or an exact provider/account pair. Expired or unreviewed versions cannot ground the active agent. Deleting a source removes its retained versions and sandbox evaluations and strips stored source content from citation metadata; it does not rewrite already-sent chat messages.

`AI_LIFECYCLE_ENABLED=true` enables runtime account profiles. Each profile has a personality and explicit keyword handoff scenarios. A sandbox evaluation makes no customer send. Activation requires a passing evaluation for the same profile revision, model/provider and source fingerprint within 24 hours. Editing relevant content/profile/model or reaching source expiry invalidates the fingerprint and causes handoff until retested. The sandbox checks the configured expected handoff outcome; it is not a comprehensive factual-accuracy or customer-satisfaction evaluation.

Website runtime consumes its profile directly. WhatsApp consumes it through the existing Sale AI planner, with both `CRM_AI_POLICY_BRIDGE_ENABLED=true` in CRM and `RRS_CRM_AI_POLICY_ENABLED=true` in Sale. Both flags default off and must only be enabled after deploying both contracts. Signed `POST /api/bridge/ai-policy` derives the exact account and latest customer message from a canonical conversation/version; it returns only matching reviewed sources, profile revision, evaluation ID and a stable policy hash. Inactive, expired, unsupported or handoff cases return no source content and cannot fall back to another account. Sale requires its mapped CRM Admin to claim the exact chat before drafting, appends the policy and approved knowledge to the native job context, and retains all existing provider/budget/consent/approval gates. The job's existing context hash detects policy changes during planning, review and sending. Its original proof travels inside `source.aiPolicy` in the approved envelope; CRM rechecks it before acceptance and at every eventual dispatch. Source revocation, account changes, new input, paused policy and turning the policy flag off block previously bound drafts. CRM creates no second social AI generator. LINE, Facebook and Instagram profiles currently support preparation/sandbox only, and the UI states that limitation.

AI analysis reports stored response outcomes, provider-reported token usage and estimated cost over 30 days. Missing cost stays missing. Time savings and ROI use visible, adjustable assumptions; they are not measured labor savings. This slice does not add a customer CSAT collection workflow.

## Authorship and website/mobile experience

New staff replies and notes record the actual member author. Reports retain current-owner workload separately from actual-author activity, so reassignment does not transfer credit for someone else's replies. Older messages without reliable author metadata remain unknown rather than being attributed to the current owner. Automation replies are excluded from human first-response attribution.

Widget settings include Thai/English language, up to six icebreakers, and required prechat consent. The server enforces consent before accepting a message when prechat is required. Resumed visitors with recorded consent do not repeat the form. Existing origin, visitor-cookie and conversation-resume protections remain applicable.

The installable web app caches only the explicit public icon/font allowlist. It does not cache HTML, APIs, chats, files, authentication or query-bearing responses. Private CRM content is not available offline. Notification permission and push delivery are not added by this slice.

## External connectors: configuration and operation

`EXTERNAL_CONNECTORS_ENABLED=true` permits Admin-managed connections. `EXTERNAL_CONNECTOR_RUNNER_ENABLED=true` enables a bounded scheduled sync of up to five accounts per wake. The worker must call `runExternalConnectors` using its organization database binding. Email dispatch additionally requires `EMAIL_SEND_ENABLED=true`; it remains separate from read access.

Configure `APP_ORIGIN`, the existing `SOCIAL_TOKEN_KEYS` keyring/current key, and each required provider's `<PROVIDER>_CLIENT_ID` / `<PROVIDER>_CLIENT_SECRET` secrets. Provider prefixes are `GMAIL`, `OUTLOOK`, `SHOPIFY`, `HUBSPOT`, `LAZADA` and `TIKTOKSHOP`. Register the exact callback `${APP_ORIGIN}/api/admin/connectors/oauth/callback`. Shopify defaults to API version `2026-10`; changing it requires contract review. TikTok additionally requires the exact seller authorization link copied from Partner Center in `TIKTOKSHOP_AUTHORIZATION_URL` (an allowed `services[.region].tiktokshop.com/open/authorize?service_id=...` URL).

The signed-in active Admin starts owner OAuth. State is single-use, expires in ten minutes and is bound to organization/member. Gmail and Outlook use PKCE. Token material is encrypted with provider/account-bound authenticated encryption and is never returned by the overview. Refresh uses a lease and persists rotated credentials. Every account control mutation advances its revision; pause followed by resume therefore fences previously approved commands. Refresh can preserve rotated credentials across such a pause without authorizing the stale send.

| Provider | Implemented operation | Readiness and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gmail | Inbox full scan, history replay/pagination, moved-into-Inbox labels, deletion tombstones, plain-text replies bound to thread | Required scopes must be returned by OAuth. New non-Inbox mail is ignored. No email file attachments. Expired history alone resets the full cursor; a deleted individual message does not. |
| Outlook | Inbox message delta with immutable IDs and validated pagination links; plain-text reply to the exact newest message | Mail.Read/User.Read and optional Mail.Send. No email file attachments. Unknown sends require provider review rather than automatic resend. |
| Shopify | Paginated order/customer reads, verified signed webhooks, replay/conflict checks, privacy redaction, explicit webhook subscription setup | Exact verified shop domain. Subscriptions recheck account revision and active Admin before every mutation. Customer data-request webhook records an owner export request; it does not automatically deliver an export. Required privacy topics must also be configured in the Shopify app. |
| HubSpot | Read-only contacts/deals plus archived/deleted records | OAuth v3 introspection verifies the actual account/scopes. No CRM writes. |
| Lazada | Owner authorization, signed token rotation, manual paginated order import from the last 30 days | Select exact country. Single-country grants only. Fixed creation window and ascending pages, 100 orders per click, bounded offset. No automatic GetOrders polling, verified push receiver, marketplace chat or cross-border shared grants. More than the bounded window can hold reports an error; it does not claim full history. |
| TikTok Shop | Seller authorization, verified authorized-shop selection, token rotation, bounded order updates with fixed time-window/page token and overlap | Seller identity and actual granted scopes required. One selected shop per rotating seller grant; multiple shops require selection and cannot independently lease the same grant. Initial history is 30 days. No marketplace chat or order writes. |
| Shopee | No adapter | Official contract access remained unavailable during implementation. This is an engineering gap as well as an owner-authorization gate. |

`connected` means owner authorization was stored; `ready` requires a successful canonical sync. A provider error, missing scope, identity mismatch or failed database transaction cannot advance the cursor or invent readiness. Imported email creates a human queue conversation and does not grant marketing consent. Commerce resources remain reference records and do not alter Sale business state. Provider account IDs scope all imported resources and email thread mappings.

Before email sending can be enabled, an Admin explicitly requests send scope and sends a self-test to that connected mailbox. Only the Inbox readback from the same mailbox with the exact test marker satisfies the proof; a Sent copy alone does not. An Admin then enables sending. Human reply commands capture the member, account revision, conversation version, recipient/thread and immutable payload hash. The dispatcher rechecks them immediately before submission, including after token refresh. The daily organization cap defaults to 100 (`EMAIL_DAILY_SEND_LIMIT`, bounded to 1–1000). Transport ambiguity becomes `unknown`; Gmail can reconcile by its stable Message-ID using a read-only Sent search. No unknown send is automatically repeated.

OAuth/permission setup and real provider delivery were not exercised by these isolated tests. No real customer messages were sent by the test suites.

## Rollout and recovery

Migrations020–025 are additive except025 rebuilding the external-account CHECK constraint while copying every existing column, then adding the unique marketplace grant binding. They must be tested on a restored production backup before rollout. Preserve ingress receipts, provider cursors, outboxes and unknown-delivery records during rollback. Disable the relevant runner/AI flags to stop new automation; do not replay unknown customer sends. Removing a connection erases its credential and stops work but retains imported history. Keep backups and encryption keys available according to the organization's retention policy.
